Privacy policy

Frameveil · Effective November 20, 2026 · Developer: Yi Wei

Your photo editing happens on your device.

Photos and recognition

Frameveil processes the images you select or capture, recognized text, faces and other suggested areas locally on your iPhone using Apple frameworks. We do not upload your images, recognized text or image metadata to our servers or an external AI service. Face detection is used to locate areas to cover, not to identify people.

Frameveil has no account system, advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK. We do not collect app usage data for advertising or tracking.

Permissions and copies

The system photo picker supplies the images you choose. Camera access is requested when you choose Take photo; permission to add to Photos is requested when saving. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Edits and exported images are made as copies. Optional embedded metadata, including location, is removed by default; Pro users can choose which supported fields to retain. The original is not overwritten. Saving to Photos or sharing sends your chosen copy to the destination you select; that service's own privacy practices then apply.

Temporary export files use iOS file protection and are excluded from backups. They are cleaned up by the app's export/session lifecycle and orphan cleanup. Copies you deliberately save or share remain in their destinations until you delete them there.

Apple purchases

Apple processes the lifetime Pro purchase. Frameveil uses StoreKit to load prices and verify purchase or restoration status on your device. We do not receive your payment-card details and do not operate a purchase-validation server. Apple may process purchase and diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and resolve the issue, not to advertise or sell personal information. Request deletion of support correspondence by emailing bamameum@gmail.com, subject to records we need to retain for applicable obligations or unresolved requests. Do not send sensitive photos or credentials.

This static support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no analytics scripts, advertising or forms. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. Opening it from the app uses your browser. This website hosting is separate from on-device photo processing.

Your choices and policy changes

You can stop editing, revoke camera/photo permissions, delete saved copies and remove the app. There is no Frameveil account to delete. We cannot access or erase copies already sent to other apps or people.

We will update this page if the app's privacy practices change and show a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact bamameum@gmail.com.

隐私政策

Frameveil · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日 · 开发者：Yi Wei

照片与识别

你选择或拍摄的图片，以及文字、人脸和其他建议选区的识别，均使用 Apple 框架在 iPhone 本机处理。我们不会将照片、识别文字或图片元数据上传至我们的服务器或外部 AI 服务。人脸检测仅用于定位遮挡区域，不用于识别个人身份。

应用不要求注册账户，不包含广告 SDK 或第三方分析 SDK，也不为广告或跟踪收集使用数据。

权限、导出和临时文件

系统照片选择器仅提供你选中的图片；拍照时请求相机权限，保存时请求向相册添加照片的权限。可在 iOS 设置中管理授权。

编辑和导出均生成副本，不覆盖原件。可选元数据和定位默认屏蔽；Pro 可选择保留支持的字段。保存或分享时，你选择的副本会发送至相应目的地，并受该服务的隐私规则约束。

临时成品采用 iOS 文件保护并排除备份，由应用的会话、导出生命周期和残留文件清理机制清理。主动保存或分享的副本会留在相应目的地，需在那里删除。

Apple 购买

终身 Pro 的付款由 Apple 处理。应用通过 StoreKit 在设备上查询价格、验证购买和恢复权益。我们不会收到银行卡资料，也没有购买验证服务器。Apple 可能按照其政策和你的设备设置处理购买与诊断信息。

支持邮件与网站

如果你主动发送支持邮件，我们会收到你提供的邮箱、邮件内容和附件，仅用于回复和处理问题，不用于广告或出售个人信息。如需删除支持往来，请联系 bamameum@gmail.com；尚未解决的问题或适用义务可能要求保留相应记录。请勿发送敏感照片或密码。

本静态支持网站托管于 GitHub Pages，不包含广告、分析脚本或表单。托管方可能为交付和保护网站而处理 IP 地址等技术请求信息。从应用打开时使用浏览器；这与照片在本机的处理相互独立。

你的选择与联系

你可以停止编辑、撤销授权、删除已保存副本或卸载应用；没有需要注销的 Frameveil 账户。我们不能删除已发送给其他应用或他人的副本。隐私实践变化时会更新本页及生效日期。问题请联系 bamameum@gmail.com。
