Zhetu helps you hide private details in photos and screenshots before sharing a copy.

Native iPhone app · English and 简体中文 · iOS 17 or later

Quick start

  1. Choose a photo or take one with the camera.
  2. Zhetu suggests and covers common private areas on your device. Tap an area or category to keep or remove its cover.
  3. Review the whole image. Automatic detection can miss information. With Pro, use manual tools to cover anything else.
  4. Preview, then save or share the edited copy. Your original photo is not overwritten.

Free and lifetime Pro

Free includes automatic privacy suggestions, reviewing suggested areas, preset watermark text, and exports up to Full HD (1920 × 1080, adjusted to the image orientation and aspect ratio).

Pro is a one-time purchase, with a US base price of US$7.99. Your App Store shows your local price before purchase. There is no recurring subscription. Pro unlocks original-size exports, manual and advanced editing, custom watermark text and style, and metadata/location controls.

To restore an existing purchase, open the membership screen and choose Restore purchases with the same Apple Account. Purchases and refund requests are handled by Apple.

Face boxes, watermarks and photo information

In the face tool, Add face creates a square in the middle of the photo. Drag it into position; double-tap it to resize. Tap Add face again for another box.

Watermarks can state an intended use. They are visible marks, not a guarantee against copying or misuse. Always review the final result.

Optional embedded metadata and location are hidden by default in exported copies. Pro lets you review fields under More and restore selected information. Dimensions and other essential decoding information remain. This cannot erase copies of your original or file dates created by a destination app.

快速使用

选择照片或拍照后，应用在本机识别并遮住常见隐私区域。点击选区或分类可以取消、补选。请检查整张图片；自动识别可能遗漏信息。Pro 可手动补充遮挡。预览后保存或分享副本，不覆盖原照片。

免费版和终身 Pro

免费版包含自动打码、检查建议选区、预设水印，以及最高 1080P 导出（适配图片方向和比例）。Pro 一次购买、终身使用，不自动续费；美国基准价为 7.99 美元，实际价格以 App Store 购买前显示的当地价格为准。Pro 解锁原尺寸导出、手动和高级编辑、自定义水印及元数据/定位控制。

恢复已购权益：使用购买时的 Apple 账户，在会员页点击“恢复购买”。付款与退款申请由 Apple 处理。

人脸框与图片信息

点击“添加人脸”后，照片中间立即出现方框，可直接拖动；双击调整大小。再次点击可继续添加。水印用于说明用途，不能保证阻止复制或滥用。

导出副本默认屏蔽可选元数据和定位。Pro 可在“更多”中逐项恢复。图片尺寸等读取所必需的信息保留；无法删除原件的其他副本，也不能阻止接收应用生成新的文件时间等信息。
