Zhetu for Android: Privacy

Effective November 26, 2026 · Developer: Yi Wei

Your images stay on your device during editing

Zhetu processes the image you choose or capture, its text, detected faces and other suggested areas on your device. We do not upload your images, recognized text, face images or embedded metadata to our servers or an external image-processing service. Face detection locates areas to cover; it does not identify people. There is no Zhetu account or advertising.

Android recognition uses Google ML Kit. Google states that input images and recognition results remain on the device. The SDK may connect to Google for models, updates and compatibility information, and sends technical diagnostics and usage metrics, including device/app information, installation identifiers, API configuration and performance. These are governed by ML Kit privacy terms, its Android data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. Zhetu does not add a separate advertising or analytics service.

Text, face and barcode models are bundled. Automatic subject selection requires a model supplied by Google Play services; it may need an initial download. Manual outlining remains available when that model cannot run.

Choosing, editing and sharing

The system picker supplies only the file you choose. Taking a picture opens your installed camera application. The camera and photo provider may apply their own permissions or remove location before supplying the file. Zhetu can show and restore only supported metadata actually present in the delivered file. You can manage system permissions in Android Settings.

Originals are not overwritten. Editing creates a fresh flattened copy. Optional embedded metadata and location are hidden by default; Pro can restore supported individual fields. Essential decoding information remains. Temporary originals are kept in the app's private cache. Only reviewed exports and camera output are exposed through narrowly scoped file-sharing permissions. App backup is disabled; temporary files are removed through session and age-based cleanup. Uninstalling removes app-private files.

Saving or sharing sends the reviewed copy to the destination you choose, such as Photos, Files or another app. Those destinations have their own privacy practices. Saved or shared copies remain there until you delete them; Zhetu cannot revoke copies already sent.

Purchases

Where enabled, Google Play processes the one-time lifetime Pro purchase. Zhetu reads store prices and purchase status, verifies signed purchase records locally and stores the verified record privately to maintain offline access. We do not receive card details or operate a purchase-verification server. Google processes transaction information under its own policies. Restoring purchases checks the same Google Play account.

Android test builds may offer an explicitly labelled test membership switch. It does not charge money or create a store purchase and is absent from production builds. If purchase configuration is unavailable, the app reports this and cannot charge you.

Support and your choices

If you email support, we receive the email, address and attachments you choose to send, to answer your request. Do not send passwords or sensitive photos. You may request deletion of support messages, subject to necessary records for unresolved requests or applicable obligations.

This static support site is hosted by GitHub Pages and contains no ads, analytics scripts or forms. The host may process technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. You can stop using the app, revoke permissions, delete copies or uninstall. Contact: bamameum@gmail.com. Material changes to these practices will be reflected here.

安卓版隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 26 日。开发者：Yi Wei。

图片与识别

遮图在设备本机处理你选择或拍摄的图片、文字、人脸和建议遮挡区域。我们不向自己的服务器或外部图片处理服务上传图片、识别文字、人脸图像或图片元数据。人脸检测只用于定位遮挡区域，不识别身份。应用没有遮图账户或广告。

安卓版使用 Google ML Kit。根据 Google 的说明，输入图片和识别结果留在本机；SDK 可能联网获取模型、更新及兼容性信息，并向 Google 发送设备与应用信息、安装标识、接口配置、性能等技术诊断和使用指标。详情见上方 ML Kit 隐私条款、Android 数据披露和 Google 隐私政策。应用没有额外接入广告或分析服务。

文字、人脸、条码模型随安装包提供。自动主体识别依赖 Google Play 服务提供的模型，首次使用可能需要下载；模型不可用时仍可手动勾画。

权限、元数据与副本

系统照片选择器仅提供所选文件。拍照会打开已安装的相机应用。相机及照片提供方可能按自己的权限规则处理文件，或在提供文件前移除定位。遮图只能显示、恢复实际收到且支持的字段；可在 Android 设置管理系统授权。

应用不覆盖原件，而是重新生成已经合成遮挡的副本。可选元数据与定位默认隐藏；Pro 可逐项恢复支持的字段，保留图片解码必需的信息。原图存于应用私有缓存，文件分享范围仅包括成品及相机输出；关闭应用备份，并按会话和文件保留时间清理临时文件。卸载会删除应用私有文件。

保存或分享时，已预览副本会发送至你选择的相册、文件位置或其他应用，并适用目的地的隐私规则。已保存或发送的副本需在目的地删除，遮图无法撤回。

购买与测试会员

启用购买时，终身 Pro 的一次性付款由 Google Play 处理。应用查询商店价格与权益，在本机验证签名购买记录，并将验证后的记录私密保存以支持离线使用。我们不接收银行卡信息，也不运行购买验证服务器。Google 按其规则处理交易信息，恢复购买使用相同的 Google Play 账户。

安卓内测版可能提供明确标注的体验会员开关；它不收费、不生成商店购买记录，正式版没有此开关。购买未配置时应用会明确提示，无法扣款。

支持与选择

你主动发送支持邮件时，我们会收到你提供的邮箱、邮件与附件，仅用于处理问题。请勿发送密码或敏感图片。可申请删除支持往来；尚未解决的问题或适用义务可能要求保留必要记录。

本静态支持网站托管于 GitHub Pages，不含广告、分析脚本或表单；托管方可能为交付及保护网站而处理 IP 地址等技术请求信息。你可停止使用、撤销授权、删除副本或卸载应用。联系邮箱：bamameum@gmail.com。隐私实践变化时会更新本页。
